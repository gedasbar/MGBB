# Interaction audit (chunk 20a, with the twelve legendary blocks)

Generated by `npm run interaction-audit` (`scripts/interaction-audit.ts`), 119 s. 36 blocks, 1260 ordered pairs (A is lower on the line, B above it), each in the three bench scenarios (big target, cluster, splitting rock) of `scripts/lib/dmgBench.ts`, 6 s per half-run, after a Flat+ on the Fan hull's middle column.

**1140 OK, 120 CANCELS, 0 EXPLODES.**

- CANCELS: the pair removes under 95% of the HP per second of the better block alone, or one block's own effect (its counter) is 0 although it happened alone.
- Blocks that cost damage on their own (below 97% of a plain Flat+ gun): Orbit, Mine, Curving Right, Random Curve, Cleaver. A pair with one of them is compared with that block alone, not with its partner alone.
- EXPLODES: over 4x the sum of the two alone, over 1,000 projectiles alive at once, or the 90th percentile update step over 4 ms (machine dependent).

Against the "before" run (`docs/interaction-audit-before.md`): 16 pairs went from a problem to OK, 2 pairs went from OK to a problem.
