# Difficulty audit (chunk 18a)

Read-only for game code: nothing in `src/` changed, no number, level, save or UI. The question: **does the content (campaign walls, Frontier, Free Fly) keep up with what a player can own at each point?** Today a Dart with one Flat+ passes up to tier 19 (the owner saw 22), and the 17a hull bot passed tier 100 with a Trident.

Rerun: `npm run difficulty-audit` (about 1 minute from the cache in `reports/difficulty-audit/`; `FORCE=1` flies every bot again, about 40 minutes on 12 cores in parallel shells, see the header of `scripts/difficulty-audit.ts`). Every table below is generated by it into `docs/difficulty-audit-tables.md` (full output, including a sensitivity table that is not copied here). Typecheck and unit tests only; no e2e was run (nothing in the game changed).

## 0. The short version

1. **Passing is not a gate.** A Mantis (the 8th hull, a multiplier build that costs 7.7K credits in all) passes tier 100; the Dart passes 19, the Twin 29, the Prong 34, Wisp / Hornet / Lancet / Kestrel / Bastion 53 (Bastion, a 2-pulse hull, stays there; Halberd and every hull from the Corvette on pass 100). Passing carves one lane, and the cheapest lane avoids the armor and the shields (doubling the armored share changes the pass need by x1.00), so armor and shields only ever make *clearing* harder.
2. **Clearing is the real pacing, and it stops growing at tier 38.** The block HP curve is flat from tier 38, so after about tier 55 nothing resists: a casual buyer with the hero level, standing at tier 60 (a Halberd with ten damage blocks at level 8, 81K credits banked) clears **all 100 tiers** with the bot. Tiers 55 to 100 are 45 tiers of walls that are already solved when you arrive.
3. **One curve cannot serve both kinds of player.** The multiplier line (a few Flat+ next to the generator, the rest Percent x) is worth x1.7 over the alternating "sensible build" at tier 20, x3.6 at 30, x8 at 60 and x32 at 100 (hero level 0). A Halberd with nine Percent x at level 5 (20.9K credits) clears 99 tiers; the sensible Zenith (1.6M credits) clears 100. The audit therefore shows **two buyers**, *casual* and *builder*, and the proposal says which one it aims at.
4. **The Frontier's 1.5% per ring passes what the best builds can do around ring 200 to 250.** Ring 100 matches a casual buyer (clear ratio 1.6), ring 200 needs 41K DPS (casual owns 9-14K, builder 177-426K), ring 400 needs 1.24M against 847K for the best build the model knows.
5. **Free Fly waves grow x1.44 a wave** (wave multiplier 1.25 times the tier step 1.055 every 15 s). The bot reaches wave 5 with the Dart, 7 with the Twin, 14-17 with the Mantis, 21-22 with the Zenith, 31-32 with the best builder (574K). The Medals at waves 40 and 50 need 46M and 2.2B DPS: out of reach for any build the game can produce today.
6. **The estimate is a ranking, not a prediction.** Builds of similar estimate clear very different walls (a Shuttle builder with an 86K estimate clears 53, a Halberd builder with 5.3K clears 99), mostly through *coverage*: a 21-wide wall must be swept, and one-ejector hulls (Wisp, Lancet, Mantis, Shuttle) stall at clear tiers 12 to 53 whatever their DPS (Mantis builder, 1.4K estimate, clears 31; Halberd sensible, 1.3K, three ejectors, clears 39). Tuning has to be checked with the bots, which is why section E flies them on the proposed walls.
7. **Other levers are barely used.** Wall speed is the same 26 px/s from tier 10 to 100; rows, armor and shields already grow; width is fixed. Section D measures each.

## 1. Method and assumptions (all of them are assumptions, none is measured on players)

- **Career axis.** A position is a campaign tier, a Frontier ring (effective tier 25 + 0.3 ring) or a Free Fly wave (the effective tier whose Frontier Free Fly square has that wave equivalent, 25 + 4 (wave - 6)). A player *arrives* at position e having played every tier before it.
- **Credits.** 3 runs per tier, each destroying 50% of the wall (`scripts/balance-damage.ts` part C), so a tier pays 1.5 x its full-clear credits; campaign to tier 100, the matching Frontier wall squares beyond. Nothing is wasted, Free Fly income is not counted. Sensitivity (x0.5 for a buyer who wastes half, x4 for a grinder) is in the tables file.
- **Medals and hero level.** One Medal per campaign tier passed, 7 Free Fly milestone Medals (one per 7 career tiers), 0.25 per Frontier ring; all of them spent on hero levels (cost round(1.2^n), +0.5 base damage a level). An upper bound: captains and options cost Medals too.
- **Two buyers with the same credits.** *Casual*: the best "sensible build" of `src/debug/hullBuilds.ts` (hull + k damage blocks, Flat+ and Percent x alternating, one level) that the credits buy, priced with the real hull, copy and upgrade prices. *Builder*: the best of that family and of the multiplier family (1-3 Flat+ nearest the generator, every other block a Percent x). Both limited to 20 damage blocks, copy caps respected.
- **DPS** is the compiler's estimate (`compile().stats.estDps`, all shots landing, hero as base damage). **Needed DPS** uses the same arithmetic as `scripts/balance-wall.ts`: *pass* = the cheapest column's effective HP (shield + HP x (1 + armor/10)) over the crossing time at the maximum boost (x4; flying low needs a quarter of it), *clear* = the wall's total effective HP over the 29 s until it reaches a ship at the bottom. Free Fly: the HP of a whole wave (splits included, every body assumed to split like a rock) over its 40 s.
- **Ratio** = owned / needed. **T** (trivial) above 5, **X** (impossible) below 0.5. A clear ratio of 1 means one run clears it, 0.5 two runs.
- **Bots** (sections C and E): `simulateWall` carve and clear-all pilots and `botRun` for Free Fly, on the real engine. A tier counts as *passed* when the carve bot passes or the clear-all bot passes or clears, *cleared* when the clear-all bot clears it; found by galloping and bisecting, then every spike and milestone tier up to the result is checked (they are the tiers that break monotony). Frontier rings: a ring counts when more than half of its up to three wall squares on the axes are won, on a coarse ring ladder.

## 6. E. Proposals

Nothing below is wired into the game. The proposal is `PROPOSED` in `scripts/lib/difficultyProposal.ts`; the script scales the measured needs by (proposed HP / today's HP) for the ratio tables and, to avoid trusting the estimate, **flies the same bots on copies of today's walls with the proposed HP** (blocks and shields scaled, whole numbers).

### 6.1 Pacing targets in plain words

Aimed at the *blend* player: the casual buyer up to about tier 40, the builder at tier 100, in between a geometric mean (question 1 below).

- **Hulls.** The Dart clears tiers 1-2; the Twin is needed from tier 3-5 and clears 5; the Prong or Wisp from tier 8-12; the Lancet / Mantis class from tier 15 to 30 (a casual buyer owns it there); a three-ejector hull with ten damage blocks (Halberd) around tier 35-50; beyond tier 55 a *casual* buyer stops clearing (53-78 even with the late hulls) and a builder reaches tiers 80-99 with a Halberd to Leviathan multiplier line. The hull prices put a new hull every 9 career tiers (17a's `POINT_STEP`); the content just has to stop being solved by the 8th.
- **Pass and clear.** Passing stays the safety net: every build that can clear tier t − 10 should still pass tier t. Clearing needs about 20 to 30 times the DPS of passing (it is x4 to x87 today, median 30), so "clear needs 2x pass" is not reachable with HP and would need a different pass rule (question 2).
- **Tier 100** needs a late hull *and* a strong build: on the proposed walls no model build clears it (the best clear 99), multiplier builds of the Mantis and the Halberd and every Shuttle-or-later build pass it, a casual buyer passes it from the Shuttle but clears only 78.
- **Frontier.** Rings 0-100 are tier 25-55 equivalents at ratio ~1 for the blend player; rings 100-250 are the campaign's tiers 55-100; beyond ring 250 the difficulty must wait for the DPS ceiling to rise (new hulls / blocks, for example the directional unlocks at rings 100/200/300). The far Frontier gets *easier* than today under this proposal, because today's compounding 1.5% passes every build the model knows around ring 200.
- **Free Fly.** Waves 1-20 as today; waves 25-50 grow x1.19, x1.08, x1.06 a wave instead of x1.44 so that wave 30 asks for a strong casual-to-builder build and wave 50 for the best builder (keep-up 0.8M against 0.85M owned).

## 7. F. Questions for the owner

1. **Who is the content for?** Recommendation: the *blend* (casual buyer until about tier 40, a player who stacks Percent x at tier 100), as proposed. Alternatives: casual only (HP x1 to x3 of today, but a multiplier builder then clears everything from tier 40 and the game's pillar "power comes from multipliers" is unrewarded), builder only (a casual buyer stops clearing at tier 31-53 and the sensible build is a dead end).
2. **Should passing become a real gate?** Recommendation: no, keep it the safety net (it unlocks the next tier and gives no Medal), and accept that tier 100 is passed by a Halberd-class multiplier line. If you want a late hull for passing, the lane has to cost more than HP can deliver (clear / pass is x30): raise `MIN_COLUMN_SHARE` from 0.3 towards 0.8 (x1.1-3.4 on passing) and then decide again. Not recommended before the HP curve is in.
3. **Do the HP change first, alone?** Recommendation: yes, a tuning chunk 18b that changes only the block HP curve (campaign tier and the Frontier formula), reruns `npm run difficulty-audit` with `FORCE=1`, and compares the bots with this document. It changes the walls of tiers 31-47 downwards (x0.6 at tier 38) and tiers 50-100 upwards; the saved progress is unaffected (tiers passed stay passed).
4. **Frontier past ring 250:** recommendation, HP x1.001 a ring until the DPS ceiling rises, and design the directional unlocks (rings 100/200/300) to give the builder x2 to x4 each, because today's 1.5% a ring is above everything the model owns from about ring 200 and would stay impossible. Alternative: keep x1.015 and accept ring 200+ as "wait for new content".
5. **Free Fly pace:** recommendation, slow the HP growth after wave 20 (x1.44 to x1.19, x1.08, x1.06 a wave). Otherwise the Medals at waves 40 and 50 are unreachable (46M and 2.2B DPS against a best of about 850K) and wave 30 is reachable only by the best builder. The proposal was **not** flown by the Free Fly bot (it needs a game change to try); the 18b chunk should.
6. **Coverage.** One-ejector hulls stall at clear tiers 12-53 whatever their DPS because a 21-wide wall must be swept. Recommendation: accept it as a hull trade-off (it is why the Halberd, three ejectors, beats the Mantis), say so in the hull picker's info text, and do not tune HP to hide it. Alternative: a spread / AoE block or wider shots.
7. **Early game:** a Dart with one Flat+ passes 19 tiers for free. Recommendation: leave it (PLAN section 4, item 7: the owner called it acceptable).
8. **Is the model good enough to tune the late curve?** Recommendation: for 18b yes, because the bots are the check, but before fine tuning tier 60-100 extend the builder family with the other blocks (status, chance, shapes, Overclock; the hill-climb search of `scripts/balance-damage.ts`), since the ceiling here is a lower bound and a human will find better lines than "2 Flat+ then 10 Percent x".

## 8. Not verified

- **Bots are not humans.** No skill, no boost play, no dodging beyond the Free Fly bot's; the carve bot never flies low; ring and tier results are one seed of a deterministic wall, and the bisection checks spike and milestone tiers but not every ordinary tier (a template can make an ordinary tier fail below the reported number).
- **The buyer model is an assumption** (3 runs a tier at 50%, nothing wasted, no Free Fly income, all Medals into hero levels, 0.25 Medals per ring, the matched-pace career axis). The sensitivity table shows what x0.5 and x4 credits do; nobody has measured real players.
- **Only Flat+ / Percent x lines on the 19 hulls (at most 20 damage blocks) are modelled.** Status, chance, shape and Mitosis blocks, Overclock and routing tricks are not, so every ceiling here is a lower bound. The position of the Flat+ blocks in the multiplier family is approximated (nearest the generator).
- **The estimate is not the engine:** builds of similar estimate clear very different walls depending on ejectors and wall shape (coverage); the ratio tables of B and E are for ranking, the bot tables for checking.
- **Free Fly:** keep-up DPS assumes every body splits like a rock; the career-axis mapping of a wave is the Frontier's; the proposed Free Fly growth was not flown by any bot, and Frontier Free Fly squares were not looked at.
- **Frontier rings:** three axis squares per ring, majority rule, a coarse ladder (rings between ladder steps are interpolated by eye); the proposal's bots scale HP of today's squares, so noise and spikes stay.
- **The proposed walls** are copies of today's walls with HP and shields multiplied and rounded, not walls the generator built; nothing was played in the browser, no e2e, no screenshots.
- **Unit tests and mutation checks:** `tests/difficultyModel.test.ts` (16 tests) covers the arithmetic (needs, credits, Medals, prices, families, proposal joins). Four mutations were made and killed: pass uses the dearest column instead of the cheapest; upgrade prices left out of the circuit cost; the first-30-tiers curve changed; the builder-family filter and the Frontier Medals removed. The tables themselves are measurements, not tested.
