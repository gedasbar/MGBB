# Tiers 101-200: frame and layout types (chunk 27a)

Design note, written before the code. The numbers are in `src/data/lateTiers.ts` (schedule, types) and `src/wall/constants.ts` / `src/wall/level.ts` (HP and
rewards); the walls are built in `src/wall/lateWalls.ts`. The owner will playtest, so every number below is a constant to change, not a rule.

## 1. Unlock and list

- Tiers 101-200 open only when **every tier 1-100 is CLEARED** (all blocks destroyed at least once), not just passed. Tier 101 needs that and nothing else; from 102 on the
  rule is the old one (the tier before is passed).
- Before that the campaign list ends with one locked entry, **"What happens after 100?"**, with the count `87 / 100 cleared`. It stands in for tiers 101-200, which are not
  shown (and not named) until the rule is met. Once it is met the entry is gone and the hundred new tiles follow tier 100 in the same list.
- Save: **no version bump.** The save already holds `clearedTiers` / `passedTiers` as plain lists of tier numbers; the loader accepted 1..100 and now accepts 1..200. A save
  from before has nothing above 100, so there is nothing to migrate (tested: the old v12 save loads unchanged, a stored 150 is kept, 201 and 0 are dropped).
- The late tiers have their own look: two new backgrounds (`galaxy_teal` for 101-150, `cave_ash` for 151-200, art language of the others), a named wall every 10th tier.

## 2. Curves

**Block HP** continues from the HP of tier 100 (about 3.4K) with three segments, `LATE_HP_SEGMENTS`:

| tiers | growth a tier | HP at the end |
|---|---|---|
| 101-130 | x1.10 | ~59K (x17) |
| 131-165 | x1.075 | ~730K |
| 166-200 | x1.06 | ~5.6M |

For comparison, tiers 41-60 grew x1.065 and 61-100 x1.0525. The segments are a fit, not the prompt's flat 1.10 (which would be x13,800 at tier 200, seven times what the
strongest build the bots could build passes: see section 7); it *starts* at 1.10 as asked.

**Rewards** per block: tier 100's reward x 1.02 per tier past 100 (`LATE_REWARD_GROWTH`; the classic curve is 1.05). The first full clear of a tier 101-200 pays **2 Medals**
(`LATE_CLEAR_MEDALS`); a captain's double-Medal chance still doubles it.

**Rows** stay at 41 (the classic curve ends at 41), so the pressure is HP, armor and shields: the shares of armored / shielded blocks go from 40% at tier 100 to 60% at tier 200.

## 3. Schedule

Ordinary tiers are just hard walls (template shapes of the classic generator, 8 of them, and the 10 named milestone walls). **38 of the 100 tiers are type tiers**, one type at a time,
never two types in one wall, never two type tiers in a row (the gaps are 2 or 3 tiers).

- **Introductions:** the nine types are introduced in this order, one every 3 tiers: 103 Monolith, 106 Maze, 109 Fortress, 112 Drift, 115 Strong Wind, 118 Crosswind, 121 Fog,
  124 Regrowth, 127 Mitosis Wall.
- **Returns:** after 127 the gap follows `2,3,2,2,3,3,2,3` repeating, and a type tier that would land on a milestone (every 10th tier) moves one tier. Each later round of nine
  is a seeded shuffle (the first type never repeats the last), so each type comes back about four times, harder each time (`rank` 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
- Chunk 27b builds Strong Wind, Crosswind, Fog, Regrowth and Mitosis Wall. Until then their 20 tiers are ordinary hard walls that keep the type (`lateType`, `typeRank`) in the
  data; the tier panel shows the name and says the effect is not active yet.

The whole table is printed by `lateTypeTiers()`; `docs/tiers-101-200-table.md` lists every tier (generated by `scripts/late-tiers-fit.ts`).

## 4. The four layout types

All rank numbers are in `lateTiers.ts` (index = rank, the last entry repeats).

- **Monolith.** 1 full-width row (rank 3-4: 2 rows, rank 5: 3 rows) of 21 blocks on the cylinder, so there is no way beside it: at least one block of each row must be broken.
  HP = `MONOLITH_HP_MUL` x the tier's block HP: **45, 50, 30 (2 rows), 35 (2 rows), 25 (3 rows)** by rank (the prompt started at x500, see section 5). No armor, no shield: single-target damage
  matters, AoE and Piercing mostly do not (their extra hits land on blocks that have nothing to lose). A monolith has few blocks, so the wall pays half of what an ordinary wall of the tier
  pays (`MONOLITH_REWARD_SHARE`, via `LevelDef.rewardMul`). Break it from low on the screen: sitting under it lifts the wall's speed (the top-edge boost) and leaves a third of the time.
- **Maze.** Every block has x1000 the tier's HP (`MAZE_HP_MUL`). A path of 3 cells (rank 3+: 2 cells) winds up the wall: straight for 1-2 rows, then a diagonal run of 3-9 rows
  moving one column a row, left or right, round the cylinder. Neighbouring rows share all but one open column, so a ship can fly it with no damage at all. Validation: a bot
  with autofire off (`simulateWall(..., 'flyer')`) flies every maze tier (`tests/lateTiers.test.ts`). Clearing needs a much later build (x1000 HP: 124M DPS at tier 106, 30B at 174).
- **Fortress.** An ordinary wall where **every** block is armored (armor 20 at rank 1-2, 25 at 3-4, 30 at 5; the engine divides damage by 1 + armor / 10) and shielded (1.25 x the block's HP at
  rank 1 up to 1.75 x), with the HP x0.65 (`FORTRESS_HP_MUL`). The classic walls top out at 50% / 50% with armor 20 and shields of 1 x HP.
- **Drift.** Rows slide sideways at their own pace while the wall comes down. 30% of every row is empty (gaps to weave through) and the wall holds 450 blocks (30 rows). Variants:
  rank 1 all rows the same direction and speed; rank 2 neighbouring rows in alternate directions; rank 3 every row its own direction and speed (40-100% of the top speed); ranks 4 and 5
  repeat alternate and each, faster. Speed 24 px/s x (1 + 0.3 (rank - 1)); a block is 54 px. The cylinder makes the slide seamless. **A lane cannot be carved** through a Drift wall
  (the blocks of every row keep coming back into it), so it asks about what clearing it asks, and its shots have to lead a target that moves 24-60 px/s over a flight of up to a second:
  HP is x0.12 / 0.10 / 0.08 / 0.07 / 0.06 the tier's (`DRIFT_HP_MUL`), tuned with the bots (section 7), which is why its blocks are far lighter than their neighbours'.
  Collision: block positions are `column x 54 + shift of the row`, wrapped; the ship's swept test, the shots' hit test, Homing, AoE and Bounce all read the moving positions.

## 5. What did not follow the prompt, and why

1. **HP growth is a fit** (section 2), not a flat x1.10: it starts at 1.10 as asked.
2. **Monolith x45 to x25, not x500.** A lane through a monolith costs `rows x mul x HP` and must be broken before the wall reaches a ship at the bottom (about 29 s). Ordinary walls of a tier ask between
   0.1 and 2.5 x HP of DPS for a pass (the median is about 1 x HP; a Slab asks most, Towers least). At x500 a one-row monolith at tier 103 asked 76K DPS where its neighbours asked 1.5K-6K, 1.6M at tier
   134 and 218M at 194: past what the strongest build tried (estimate 2.8M) can do from about tier 134. x45 asks 1.5 x HP at rank 1 up to 2.5 x HP at rank 5.
3. **Drift is far lighter than its neighbours** (section 4): a lane cannot be carved there.
4. **Fortress HP x0.65**: the bots (section 7) found x1 + armor 20-40 + shields of 1.5-2.5 x HP past what the ordinary walls of its tiers ask.
5. There are no Spike tiers past 100; the type tiers take their place.
6. Tiers 101-200 do not touch the Frontier (its HP curve still reads tier 100 as its ceiling).

## 6. Where it plugs in

`generateLevel(tier)` (generator.ts) hands tiers above 100 to `buildLateLevel`. `MAX_TIER` is 200, `CLASSIC_TIERS` 100 (the audit scripts and the spike checks still mean the first hundred).
`isUnlocked` / `nextTier` (`econ/progress.ts`) carry the cleared rule. `WallWorld` keeps a shift per row (zero for every wall but Drift) that every lookup (cell under a point, the
block field, the swept ship test) subtracts. `applyWallRun` pays `LATE_CLEAR_MEDALS`. The Map screen draws the locked entry or the hundred new tiles, with the type's name on the tile
and its line in the panel.

## 7. Fit and bot report

Made with `scripts/late-tiers-fit.ts` (`PART=bots`, `PART=reach`; the real engine flown by the carve and clear-all bots of `src/wall/sim.ts`, the same ones the audits use, 60 steps a second). Three builds, all with hero level 21
(base damage 11.5), none limited by what they would cost:

| build | what | estimate |
|---|---|---|
| A | the difficulty model's best (Leviathan, 2 Flat+ + 10 Percent x L10) | 25K DPS |
| B | tall hull, 3 Flat+ L10 + 10 Percent x L10, no +1 Projectile | 504K DPS |
| C | the same with 6 +1 Projectile first | 2.8M DPS |

**Highest tier in 101-200, strict order** (every tier on the way must be passed; `PART=reach`, ordinary tiers by bisection, every built type tier checked):

| build | passes in strict order up to | ordinary tiers up to | clears (ordinary tiers) up to | built type tiers it fails below that |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | about 140 | | none (cannot clear tier 101) | |
| B | **175** | 188 | 123 | 176 Drift, 179 Fortress |
| C | **193** | 198 | 140 | 194 Monolith |

(Monolith and Maze tiers cannot be cleared by design: the Maze has x1000 HP and the Monolith is a lane of one block a row; the "clears" column is about ordinary tiers (the clear-all bot, and the built type tiers below that limit that it can clear were all cleared). The ordinary-tier limits come from a bisection over templates whose cost differs by 100x, so they are only good to about 10 tiers: B passes 170 and not 180,
and its bisection said 188.)

Reading it: tier 101 is passed by a build of the model's best kind and cleared by nobody below B; B (13 damage blocks, no extra shots) walks to about tier 175 and clears up to 123;
C (six extra shots) reaches 193 and no build tried passes tier 200. The type tiers are the gates only in their late ranks (Drift 176 and Fortress 179 for B, Monolith 194 for C), as intended:
they are "a little harder than the neighbours", not a wall in front of every build.

**What the bots did not tell:** the carve bot cannot play Drift (it crashes within 2 s: a lane cannot be carved), so Drift was fitted with the clear-all bot only; both bots are blunt
(they never weave through gaps, never dodge, never use the ship's speed), so a human may pass what they could not and fail what they passed. Maze was fitted by the zero-damage
`flyer` pilot, which passes every maze tier (a unit test). The five 27b types are not in this table.

**What the first fit looked like (kept for the next one):** a flat x1.10 over the hundred tiers would make tier 200's blocks 13,800 x tier 100's (the strongest build passes about x1,840);
Monolith x500 asked 16 x HP of DPS; Fortress at armor 20-40 and shields 1.5-2.5 x HP failed build B at 142, 167 and 179; Drift at x0.35 HP and 450 blocks failed B at 144, 154 and 176.
