# Circuit builder rebuild: final report

Everything below was produced in one session. Test names are exact; screenshots are in `e2e/screenshots/` (generated by the Playwright scripts, git-ignored). Coordinates are `(col,row)`, 1-indexed, as in the hull diagram.

## Summary

* The red line is automatic. The generator is a fixed hull cell, the line exists on an empty grid, runs through empty cells as implicit straight pieces, and only changes at blocks. Dead ends are drawn with a cross or warning triangle and counted as warnings.
* Blocks follow the line (auto mode, green "A" badge) or are locked by hand (padlock). A locked block only accepts a signal travelling the way it faces.
* The old palette is gone. Double-tap an empty cell to open the bottom-sheet picker; single tap previews (ghost block, re-routed line, stats with deltas), double tap places.
* The picker offers **14 of 62 blocks**, each proven to compile correctly and to change what the ship fires.
* Stages 1-6 of the build order are all finished. The project builds, typechecks and passes every test.

**One deviation from the brief, flagged first:** the brief's default circuit (Turn Right at (6,9), Flat+ at (9,6)) cannot reach the ejector, because column 10 is void and a line turning right at (6,9) dead-ends at (10,9). The default circuit therefore also contains a **Turn Left at (9,9)**. All tests use these three blocks. See DECISIONS.md #7.

## 6. Decisions and what is not finished

The full list is `DECISIONS.md` items 7-22. The ones that matter most:

* The default circuit has a second turn (see the top of this file).
* Chance blocks are rolled per projectile in `World.fire` (the only `World` change); the stats strip uses their average.
* Piercing, Shield-Breaker and Armor-Piercer have engine quirks that I documented rather than changed (Rule 4). Armor-Piercer stays hidden.
* Inventory is 20 per block, available separately to circuits A and B.
* The ship button opens a small hull picker (Shuttle, Fan); each hull keeps its own A and B circuits.

Not finished or worth knowing:

* **48 blocks are hidden** (mitosis, status, passives, most path blocks). They stay in the data with a reason each, and need engine support before they can be offered.
* **`World` spawns every projectile at the ship's centre**, whatever ejector it came from, so several streams overlap on screen. The count and damage per pulse match the strip. Spreading them by ejector position is a Fly change I left alone.
* Pinch zoom and touch long-press are unit-tested with simulated pointers but not driven in a real browser (Playwright's mouse cannot make touches). Wheel zoom, drag, hover and mouse long-press are tested in the browser.
* Shop, Captain, Skills and Map tabs are stubs; the currency readout is a placeholder.
* An old-format save is migrated by raw cell coordinates onto the new Shuttle, so migrated blocks may not form a working circuit (they land in slot A and the player can clear them).
* Copy/Paste needs clipboard permission; without it the player gets a toast.
